How it works

  1. Enter the article

    Paste the headline and the article text you want to review.

  2. Text is analyzed

    The app cleans the text and passes it to a trained classification model.

  3. Read the classification

Important limitations

A classification result is not proof of truth or falsehood. The model learns patterns from its training dataset and can make mistakes, particularly on unfamiliar topics, new events, or sources it has not seen before.
