Test 2 – Technology

Italy fines Meta $887 million over Facebook's access to Android users' photos

Italy's data protection authority fined Meta Platforms 805 million euros, about $887 million, after finding that a Facebook feature on Android accessed users' photos and videos without specific consent. The feature suggested images from a phone's gallery for posting and was switched on by default, affecting roughly 20 million Android users in Italy, according to the regulator. Meta has 60 days to disable the feature by default and ask users for separate consent. The company called the fine disproportionate and said it will appeal. The investigation, which began in 2025, did not examine whether the content was used to train AI models, a practice Meta denies.
